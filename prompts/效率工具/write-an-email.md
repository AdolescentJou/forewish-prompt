# Write an Email

## 说明

用途概述：Write a ${tone:professional|friendly} email to ${recipient} about ${topic}.
中文概述：按语气(正式/友好)、字数与语言要求为指定收件人撰写含明确行动号召的英文邮件。
关键词：email writing、professional tone、call to action、邮件生成

## 元信息（仓库提供）

- 来源仓库：[f/prompts.chat](https://github.com/f/prompts.chat)
- 贡献者：training002025@gmail.com
- 类型：纯文本（原始类型 TEXT）
- 面向开发者：否

## Prompt 内容

```text
Write a ${tone:professional|friendly} email to ${recipient} about ${topic}.

The email should:
- Be approximately ${length:200} words
- Include a clear call to action
- Use ${language:English} language
```
