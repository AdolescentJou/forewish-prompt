# Revenue Performance Report

## 说明

用途概述：Generate a monthly revenue performance report showing MRR, number of active subscriptions, and churned subscriptions for the last 6 months, grouped by month.
中文概述：生成近 6 个月按月分组的营收业绩报表，展示 MRR、活跃订阅数与流失订阅数。
关键词：MRR、revenue report、订阅、报表
