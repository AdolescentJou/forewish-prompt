# Step 4b: Story Development

## 说明

用途概述：Develop the full story and content based on the creative exploration.
中文概述：基于前期创意探索展开完整故事，产出叙事弧、角色、场景、对白与情感节拍
关键词：故事创作、叙事弧、角色塑造、对白、情感节拍

## 元信息（仓库提供）

- 来源仓库：[f/prompts.chat](https://github.com/f/prompts.chat)
- 贡献者：[@f](https://github.com/f)
- 类型：纯文本（原始类型 TEXT）
- 面向开发者：否

## Prompt 内容

```text
Develop the full story and content based on the creative exploration.

Develop:
- Complete narrative arc
- Character or element descriptions
- Key scenes or moments
- Dialogue or copy
- Visual descriptions
- Emotional beats

Create compelling, engaging content.
```
